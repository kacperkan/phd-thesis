\section*{Contributions}
  As our articles are the fruits of a collaborative effort, I list my
  contributions to each of the articles in~\cref{tab:publications-in-thesis}.

  \paragraph{CoNeRF: Controllable Neural Radiance Fields.}
    \begin{itemize}
      \item I introduced the idea of using sparse annotations as a control signal
            for learning controllable radiance fields.
      \item I created all the datasets used in the published article.
      \item I designed the final CoNeRF architecture.
      \item I conducted the experiments reported in the submission and wrote the
            initial version of the article.
    \end{itemize}

  \paragraph{BlendFields: Few-Shot Example-Driven Facial Modeling.}
    \begin{itemize}
      \item I designed and performed early experiments that revealed the
            limitations of current condition-based approaches for modeling realistic
            facial wrinkles in a few-shot setting.
      \item Motivated by my collaborators, I began exploring the use of wrinkle-
            maps~\cite{oat2007animated} in the context of neural radiance fields.
      \item I devised the branched structure of the architecture employed in the work.
      \item I carried out the experiments presented in the submission, wrote the
            initial version of the article, and assisted in its refinement.
    \end{itemize}

  \paragraph{LumiGauss: High-Fidelity Outdoor Relighting with 2D Gaussian Splatting.}
    \begin{itemize}
      \item I partially supervised the project and helped design the final architecture
            comprising \lumigauss.
      \item I authored the revised version of the paper, corrected figures, and
            oversaw further improvements.
    \end{itemize}

  \paragraph{CLoG: Leveraging UV Space for Continuous Levels of Detail.}
    \begin{itemize}
      \item I designed and performed the initial experiments that helped us identify
            the main research goal.
      \item I created the complete architecture and training regime for \clog.
      \item I executed all the experiments reported in the submitted paper, wrote
            the article, and assisted in its refinement.
    \end{itemize}
